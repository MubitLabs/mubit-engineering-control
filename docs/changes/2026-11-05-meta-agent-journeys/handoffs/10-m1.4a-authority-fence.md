## Outcome

- The owner can hold, lift, stop, pause and resume over HTTP and from the classic UI.
- A stop during a planner turn means nothing launches, ever. An old question or timer cannot revive stopped work.
- A hold survives restart and only the owner's lift command releases it. The planner cannot lift it, even from chat.
- A plan finishing during a hold is saved with zero launches and launches on lift.
- Pause survives restart, shows at startup, and starts no effects, including during shutdown.

**State changes go through the machines**

4. `#stopPlanningWork` sets `stopped` from any state, including `done`. Replace it with one path using `transition(WORK_ITEM_MACHINE, state, 'stop')`, with `withdraw` applied to the mandate in the same transaction.
5. `#failTurn` and the plan-rejection branch of `#commitPlan` stop work only if its state is `planning`. A failed wake on `executing` work leaves it running and records `meta_agent_error` only.
6. The commit step of `#commitPlan` sets `work.state = 'executing'` directly (`service.ts:612`). Use `transition`, and handle a replan on work already `executing` (M1.2 #19 path) without throwing.

**Commit fence (§10, §13 invariant 4)**

7. Inside the commit `atomic` of `#commitPlan`, and in `#ackOnlyTurn`, re-read the work doc after the brain returns. The brain `await` is the race window.
   - Terminal or mandate withdrawn: discard the plan and every extra (questions, confirmations, routes, spawns). Save only the message with a truthful `ackText` saying it was stopped and nothing will run. Enqueue nothing.
   - Held (#20): save tasks and the plan, enqueue zero launches, create zero attempts, keep `doc.hold`. The ack says it is held.
8. `onWake` with a `workItemId` returns before calling the brain when the work is terminal or its mandate is withdrawn. This fixes the case where a question answered on stopped work moves it to `executing` and enqueues a launch (`wave0-m1.md` probe D). No quota is spent.
9. Extract the dispatch block of `#commitPlan` (`ready` through the launch loop) into a private method usable by lift, in its M1.2 form. Do not change its dependency rules. Continuing scheduling is M2.2.

**Holds (#20, §12.5, §26 Hold)**

10. Remove planner `release` everywhere: the `decision.routes` branch in `#persistExtras` (`service.ts:861-866`), `case 'release'` and `needsTarget` in `packages/meta/src/tools.ts:249,281-285,288`, the enum and description in `packages/mec-tools/src/tools.ts:144,151`, and the `routes` comment at `service.ts:83`. 12. New service commands, each one transaction with its event: `holdWork(id, reason, source)`, `liftHold(id)`, `stopWork(id)`.
    - `liftHold` runs `revalidate` first (§10 "before every resume"), lifts, then calls the dispatch from 9 for ready tasks with no live attempt. Lift on a free item returns `free` and writes nothing.
    - `stopWork` moves queued attempts of that work to `interrupted` and appends `attempt.interrupt_requested` for running ones. Requires one new machine row, see 13. Process kill is M2.1.
13. `ATTEMPT_MACHINE` has no exit from `queued` (`packages/domain/src/states.ts`), and `packages/domain/test/states.test.ts:118` lists the table. Add `{ from: 'queued', event: 'interrupt_confirmed', to: 'interrupted' }`: nothing is running, so the interrupt is confirmed at once. That test changes on purpose; say so in your record. Not a contracts change.

**Launch gate (§13, §20)**

14. `launchHandler` (`composition.ts:366`) calls `launchAuthority` before anything else, fake path included:
    - `drop`, returning normally with a `launch_refused` event: work missing or terminal, mandate withdrawn or expired, repo not matching the pin, or attempt not `queued`. The last one matters: `#replay` re-enqueues launch keys, and a drained row is deleted, so a late replay makes a new row.
    - `defer`, throwing `Later`: work held. Already-queued launches restart within the 30 s `LATER_DELAY_SECONDS` after a lift. Do not try to shorten this: `upsert` keeps `next_run_at`, although §17 says re-enqueue makes it due at once. Note the mismatch in your record.
15. `policy_changed`: no launch, see open question.

## Open questions

- **§10, mandate `scope` and `budgetTurns`, and default expiry.** Nothing defines them. Recommend `scope` as the first 200 characters of the origin message, `budgetTurns: 0` meaning unset, and `expiresAt: null`. Nothing enforces them yet. Ask before enforcing.
- **§13, `policy_changed` at launch.** §13 says a change never cancels live work and revalidate runs before a resume, but a queued launch is neither clearly. Policy text is fixed at boot today, so this only happens across a restart with a new file. Recommend `drop` with a `launch_refused` reason, and the work blocked with `policy_changed`, since the mandates module says it continues only after a clean revalidation.
- **§12.1 and §12.5, planner `stop` and `hold` on a wake turn.** Spec says "the user's", but never says a wake turn is refused. Recommend refusing, as in requirement 11.
- **§12.5 versus #20, "the user's explicit go".** #20 resolves it to the lift command, so a chat "go" no longer lifts. The spec wording needs the change proposed above.
- **§10, legacy Unassigned work.** Read-only in M1.2, but §13 invariant 4 protects a human stop. Recommend allowing stop on legacy rows and refusing hold and lift.
- **§20, "a message to an ended item waits and resumes it on the first pass after the pause if its mandate holds".** Not implemented, and it reads awkwardly against §12.1. Recommend leaving it to M2.4.
